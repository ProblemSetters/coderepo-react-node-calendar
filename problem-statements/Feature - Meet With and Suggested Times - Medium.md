# Feature: Meet With and Suggested Times

`Medium`

## Overview

**Skills:** React, Node.js, Scheduling Algorithms (Intermediate), Code Repository
**Recommended Duration:** 75 minutes

Implement collaborative availability comparison and conflict-aware meeting scheduling.

## Product Requirements

- Search people by name or email with complete keyboard support.
- Compare You and up to ten people in scrollable Day columns.
- Find suggestions inside shared working hours without conflicts.
- Keep local dates correct across UTC offsets and daylight-saving transitions.
- Warn about conflicts while editing and recheck before save.

## Steps to Test Functionality

- Add and remove multiple people and inspect comparison columns.
- Test durations, fully booked days, suggested-time handoff, and conflicting meetings.
